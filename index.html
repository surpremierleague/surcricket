      About the SPL Committee

      We formed the SPL Committee to take sport in Sur to the next level and to build a strong sporting community.

      What we do

      The SPL Committee exists to raise the standard of sport in Sur. Through Sur Premier League Season 2 and Season 3 we gave local teams and players a proper stage to compete. Now we are taking the next step. Our upcoming tournament is built to bring bigger challenges and better cricket for the whole community.

      This site keeps our tournaments, teams and player records in one place, so every player and team can see how they are doing.

      How stats are worked out

      Bat avgRuns ÷ (innings − not outs)
      Strike rateRuns per 100 balls faced
      EconomyRuns conceded per over
      Bowl avgRuns conceded ÷ wickets
      BestMost wickets in a single match

      Spotted a mistake?
