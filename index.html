Sur cricket player stats

Combined matches, runs and wickets = the earlier stats list + four CricHeroes tournaments. The earlier list only has matches, runs and wickets, so averages, strike rate, economy and fielding come from the tournaments alone. Open any row for the full card.

Bat avg = runs ÷ (innings − not outs). Strike rate = runs per 100 balls. Economy = runs conceded per over. Best = most wickets in one match. A dash means no data.
